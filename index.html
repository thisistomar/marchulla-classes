ABOUT MARCHULLA CLASSES

Purposeful preparation.
Personal guidance.

MARCHULLA Classes is an offline civil services coaching and mentoring initiative for aspirants preparing for UPSC Civil Services and State PSC examinations.

The approach emphasizes disciplined preparation, conceptual understanding, analytical thinking and sustained mentoring. Strategic exam guidance is complemented by academic depth in answer writing, current affairs, governance, economy, agriculture, environment and rural development.
