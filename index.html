About Us

Purposeful preparation. Personal guidance.

MARCHULLA Classes is an offline civil services coaching and mentoring initiative for aspirants preparing for UPSC Civil Services and State PSC examinations. The academic approach emphasizes disciplined preparation, conceptual understanding, analytical thinking and sustained mentoring.

The institute brings together strategic exam guidance and academic depth, with attention to answer writing, current affairs, governance, economy, agriculture, environment and rural development.
